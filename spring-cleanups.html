Why a spring cleanup matters

A thorough cleanup early in the season pays off all year long.

  • Healthier lawn. Clearing leaves and thatch prevents mold, snow mold damage, and bare patches.
  • Fewer weeds. Cleaning and mulching beds early stops weeds before they get established.
  • Stronger plants. Proper cutbacks and pruning encourage vigorous new growth and better blooms.
  • Pest prevention. Removing debris eliminates hiding places for ticks, insects, and rodents.
  • Instant curb appeal. A clean, edged, freshly mulched yard looks great from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Just ask when you request your quote.

When is the best time for a spring cleanup?

Usually as soon as the snow is gone and the ground has firmed up enough to work on without damaging the lawn — typically early to mid spring.

How long does a cleanup take?

Most residential cleanups are finished in a single visit. Larger properties or yards with heavy debris may take a bit longer; we'll let you know when we quote the job.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can access the yard, you don't need to be there. We'll let you know when the work is done.

What do you do with the debris?

We bag or load all leaves, branches, and clippings and haul them away, so there's nothing left at the curb.

Can I add mulch or other services?

Absolutely. Mulching, dethatching, the first mow, and other services can be added to your cleanup. Just check them off on the quote form.
